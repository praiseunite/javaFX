On this page

Before Week 1 — the Java Foundations Bridge

Seven short self-paced lessons that get every student to the same starting line. They are pre-work: work through them before the first class if you are new to Java, and skim them as revision if you are not. Nobody is graded on the Bridge, but everything after it assumes it.

BridgeLessonWhat you can do afterwards
B1Setup: JDK & IntelliJInstall the tools, run your first program, read a compiler error without panicking
B2Variables, Types & OperatorsStore numbers and text, and predict what an expression evaluates to
B3Decisions & LoopsMake the program choose with if, and repeat with for/while
B4Methods & ArraysBreak a program into methods, and hold many values in an array
B5Classes & ObjectsWrite your own type, with fields and constructors
B6Inheritance & InterfacesReuse code with extends, and promise behaviour with implements
B7Strings, Exceptions & PackagesHandle text safely, catch failures, and organise code into packages

Session numbers run 1–18 with a review after every block: three reviews in total. The three reviews are the days your assignments are marked, so they are not free periods — come with your work finished and your questions ready.

What a 2-Hour Session Looks Like

Every session page follows the same rhythm, and every class period does too. The shape is deliberate: you meet an idea, you see it run, you do it yourself, and you find out what you got wrong — all before you leave the room.

TimeBlockYou are…
0–10 minWarm-upAnswering last session's questions and a quick exit-ticket check
10–35 minConceptReading the idea, the analogy and the first worked example
35–55 minGuided practiceTyping the example along with your teacher, and breaking it on purpose
55–65 min☕ BreakResting — genuinely, it helps
65–105 minThe Guided LabWorking through the checklist yourself, with your teacher circulating
105–115 minKnowledge CheckAnswering the quiz and reading the explanation for every answer
115–120 minExit ticketWriting down the one thing still unclear — that is what opens the next class

✦
Every assignment is marked on the same four dimensions

Correctness (does it work?), Design (is the structure right?), Style (naming, formatting, readability) and Documentation & Tests (comments, Javadoc, tests). A program that runs but nobody can read does not score full marks. The full brief for each one is on the Assignments page.
